GOOD TO KNOW

A few honest answers.

Is Lore a real astrologer?

Lore is an AI companion that uses astrology and numerology as symbolic traditions for reflection. She isn't a human astrologer, and her messages aren't predictions or professional advice.

Can Lore guarantee I'll reach my goals?

No. Lore can help you reflect, break a goal into smaller steps and notice your own progress. What you choose to do remains up to you.

What happens to my birth date?

Your birth details are used to personalise your experience in Telegram. Review the Privacy page before sharing information, and only share what you're comfortable providing.
