Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

This note describes how It's My Lore uses the data you share when you chat with Lore in Telegram. It's My Lore is a product operated by Rubyness Ltd, a company registered in Scotland under company number SC729112 (“Rubyness”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

Who to contact

For any privacy or data question about Lore: natali@rubyness.co.uk.

What we collect

When you chat with Lore in Telegram we store, on our server:

  • your Telegram user ID, username (if you have one) and first name — Telegram gives these to any bot you open;
  • anything you tell Lore in the chat, including your birth date if you share it for your Lore;
  • your trial and subscription status (start, end, renewal);
  • technical logs (timestamps, message IDs) needed to run the service.

We do not ask for and we do not collect your phone number, address, photos, contact list or location.

Why we use it

  • to reply to you personally — Lore remembers your name, your birth date and what you told her before;
  • to run the subscription (start your trial, show your status, process your payment);
  • to keep the service running (error logs, abuse prevention).

We do not sell your data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it to train public AI models.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic — Lore's replies are written by AI through Anthropic's Claude API. Your messages are sent there so Lore can answer. Anthropic processes them under its own terms and does not use them to train its public models.
  • Telegram — the chat itself and payments (Telegram Stars) go through Telegram under its own terms.
  • Our server provider — Lore runs on a server managed for us by Cybergizer.

We don't share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as you use Lore. If you send /forget in the chat, Lore wipes everything she remembers about you. If you want your account fully removed, write to natali@rubyness.co.uk.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you are in the UK, EU or EEA you can also complain to your data-protection regulator (in the UK: the ICO, ico.org.uk).

Age

Lore is for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please don't use her.

Cookies and analytics on this landing page

This website uses Google Tag Manager (GTM-NJ2HK5VQ) and loads Google Fonts from Google's servers. The chat with Lore in Telegram does not use cookies.

Changes

If we change this notice we'll update the date at the top. Material changes we'll mention in Lore's chat too.
